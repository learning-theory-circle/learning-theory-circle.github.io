## Learning Theory Circle

We are a community of students and researchers interested in theoretical foundations of machine learning. We have a biweekly gathering, and at each session, one person presents a paper on the theory of learning, either their own work or someone else's, followed by open discussion. The emphasis is on understanding and open discussion rather than polished talks. The talks and recordings are in Persian for now. You are welcome to join us in the discussions or presentations. See the [About](about) page for more details on the format, and how to present. Announcements go out on our [mailing list]({{ site.mailing_list_url }}), and recordings live on [YouTube]({{ site.youtube_playlist_url }}).


## When and Where

Every two weeks on Sunday, 7:00 pm Tehran time, or equivalently UTC+3:30, we meet on [Google Meet]({{ site.meet_url }}). You can add the event to your [Google Calendar]({{ site.google_calendar_url }}).
